Why rereading fails § 02

Rereading is recognition. Recall is knowledge.

Familiarity feels like knowing. The real question is whether the words are still there once the page is gone.

1On the page

No person shall be deprived of his life or personal liberty except according to procedure established by law.

Every word is right there.

Five words gone. The only test that counts.
