## 1. What is wrong today (verified at `5d89fbd`, unchanged by M1)

- `web/guest_bareact_head.py` is a fixture: constant title, kicker, meta "6 Chapters · Sections 1–83 · Schedule" and three constant rows rendered as `div`s with no link. `bare_act.html` uses it for guests **and** signed-in Free users on `/laws/ndps` only (`guest_screen4 = ndps_guest_head() if bare.slug == "ndps" else None`). The live model already derives the right label (`BareAct.meta_label` → "8 Chapters · Sections 1–83 · Schedule").
- Every other eligible Act (`/laws/uapa`, `/laws/bns`, `/laws/bnss`, `/laws/pss`, `/laws/mtp`) renders the legacy full panel: "Bare Act" tag, title, Playground CTA, "About this act" disclosure, Chapters/Schedules tab row (hidden until JS), accordion with Chapter I open, schedule rows.
- `bare_act.html` carries six surface variants (guest desktop, guest phone, signed-in desktop, signed-in Free phone, Plus phone, the full panel) with per-surface CSS in `styles.css` / `mobile.css` / `playground.css`.
- The Add sheet (`playground_add.html`, opened in-page through `dialog.pg-sheet` by `playground.js` from `data-pg-sheet` triggers, or rendered as a page on direct navigation) has guest / Free / Plus / expired / halted variants; opened by URL it is a standalone page with the legacy footer (DV-15). Copy for Free reads "Unlock Playground"; the prototype reads "Add to Playground" with a lede naming the Act and a footnote listing the plans.

## 4. Decisions the owner must make before implementation

1. **Chapter-count rule, applied everywhere.** Direction says eight entries for NDPS. The catalogue card (`guest_laws_index.py`, M3) and the fixture say six. M2 proposes: the head always prints `act.meta_label` (derived; eight for NDPS), and M3 replaces the guest Laws fixture so the card prints the same derived label. Confirm.
2. **About-this-Act placement.** Prototype head has no About block. Options: (a) keep the existing "About this act" disclosure under the CTA (function preserved, small visual deviation); (b) move it to a row after the schedules; (c) a secondary link next to the meta line. Proposal: (a).
3. **Chapter row navigation.** Chapters have no page of their own. Options: (a) row expands in place to list its sections (today's accordion behaviour, restyled to the prototype row); (b) row links to the chapter's first section. Proposal: (a) on both widths, with the chevron turning into a caret when open; the schedule row links to the schedule page.
4. **Add sheet by direct URL on desktop.** (a) render the Act page underneath and auto-open the modal; (b) a centred card on the shared shell. Proposal: (a) when the referrer/route allows, else (b).
