M3 is split accordingly: layout, catalogue, search, chip and count corrections proceed on their own; every banner or action that states an entitlement waits for the billing module's actual behaviour or is shipped with its **existing, enforced** copy and recorded as PENDING-ENTITLEMENT. M3 must not display the new promises while staging still enforces the old rules, and must not imply an Article restriction in any copy it introduces.

## 1. What is wrong today (verified at `3719b9f`; bodies unchanged by M1 and M2)

- **Browse phone** (`browse_index.html` + `mobile.css`): the phone shows `mobile-screen-head` ("Browse", a search icon to `/search`, and a "Sign in" chip for guests or a "Marks" sheet button for signed-in users). The phone stylesheet hides the lede, the guest strip, the free-plan summary, the MARKS legend and every `browse-part` section, and shows instead the `browse-part-rail` — Part cards with roman numeral, name, range, a learned label and a progress bar (`stg_phone_browse.png`). Prototype (`proto_phone_browse_guest.png`, `proto_phone_browse_signedin.png`): title "Browse the Constitution" with the lede; guest banner "Reading as a guest. Sign in to save 3 Articles to Recall for free." with a black Sign in button, or the free-plan line "Free plan — pick any 3 Articles. All 3 slots free." with "Unlock all" (both banner copies are superseded by the entitlement model, §2.3 — layout reference only); Laws / Tables cards; MARKS legend (In news, Visualise); "THE CONSTITUTION" label; Part headers (Part I · The Union and its territory · Articles 1–4 · chevron) that collapse; a two-column article card grid (number + title) under each Part.
- **Browse desktop, signed-in**: the free-plan summary renders when `current_user and not plus_browse and not expired_browse and not halted_browse` — i.e. for **Pro and Max** too (only active Plus is excluded), as the M1 paid pack showed on Plus `browse_1280` ("Free plan — pick any 3 Articles… Unlock all" for a subscriber). Prototype: the banner belongs to Free accounts only. Printing a Free banner to a subscriber is wrong under the old model and the new one, so the audience fix is independent of the billing module; the banner's copy is not (§2.3).
- **Laws index, guest** (`laws.html` first branch, `guest_laws_index` = multi-user and no `current_user`, both widths): fed by the fixture `web/guest_laws_index.py` (`GUEST_LAWS_CARDS`, `GUEST_LAWS_CHIPS`) with typed meta — NDPS "6 Chapters · Sections 1–83 · Schedule", UAPA "7 Chapters · Sections 1–53" (no "· Schedule") — and a chip set (Commercial, Financial, …) that does not follow the catalogue (S03: staging has Health, no Commercial). Prototype cards read "8 Chapters · Sections 1–83 · Schedule" and "7 Chapters · Sections 1–53 · Schedule"; the Act head already derives those (`BareAct.head_meta_label`, M2).
- **Laws index, signed-in phone** (`laws.html` second branch): legacy `laws-index-head` ("← Browse", "Laws", a search **toggle icon**; the field is hidden until toggled), chips from the catalogue, and every eligible card carries `playground_cta(pg_state)` — for Free: "Unlock Playground · from ₹199 / month." + "Subscribe to use Playground" (`stg_phone_laws_signedin.png`, S11). Prototype (`proto_phone_laws_signedin.png`): "← Browse", kicker BROWSE, title "Laws", lede "Bare Acts and statutes. Open a law, then add it or its sections to your Playground.", a visible search field, chips, cards with tag line, chevron, title and meta only — the CTA lives on the Act head (M2).
- **Laws index, desktop**: matches the prototype for guest and signed-in (D3, S03) apart from the card meta above; regression rows only.
- **`/browse/part/{slug}` phone** (`browse_part.html`): legacy list page ("← All Parts", Part title, progress, article rows with Learned/Preview markers). The prototype has no Part screen (its `screen=` list has no `part`); the prototype folds Parts into collapsible groups on Browse. Under §0.2a the page stays reachable (§4 Q1).

### 2.3 Pending on the billing module (entitlement-model coordination)
