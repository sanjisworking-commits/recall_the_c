## 1. What M7 is

Two documents define it:

- Roadmap M7, "Subscribed Playground flows" (`PLAYGROUND_FIDELITY_ROADMAP.md` §2, PR #218): `/playground` (laws present, empty), `/playground/laws/{id}`, `…/sections`, Learn (six modes), `…/learned`, `…/mastered`, `/playground/roster`, `/playground/roster/next`; the picker defect; DV-17 rows, never compared.
- Tier-revision plan M7-R (§2 D11, D12, D18, D23; §3.5; §4.5): home, Manage laws and Remove without the month model; the Free counter and "Previously chosen"; Plus / Pro "Unlimited"; rollover retired from navigation (5-23). Handover frames `desktop-12/13/19`, `phone-11/12/19`.

`monthly` is the active model and month copy is correct there. `choices` is built but inactive, and on these pages it is unfinished. M7 is a fidelity pass on the flows a subscriber uses today plus the `choices` branches of the pages that still speak in months, leaving what `monthly` accounts see unchanged except where a defect is decided for fixing.

**Out.** Ended-subscription transitions and the paid→Free downgrade roster (5-20 → M8-R); device edge states (M8); prices (5-1); the Plus / Pro confirm step (5-14); revision-date copy on re-add (5-12); any entitlement, billing, ledger or migration change.
